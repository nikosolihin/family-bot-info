Privacy policy

Family Bot is a private tool used by one family only.

  • It uses Google sign-in only to create, change, and delete events in that family's Google Calendar, and to-dos in its Google Tasks.
  • It reads the family's calendar list only to find the calendar to write to.
  • It does not sell, share, or send any data to anyone else.
  • Its sign-in token is stored only on the family's own computer. The family can remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
